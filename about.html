About Prufrock Builders

Built With Precision, From the Ground Up.

Prufrock Builders is a full-service construction company specializing in ground-up construction and whole-home remodeling. From new builds framed for the future to renovations that transform the everyday, we bring precision, craftsmanship, and clear communication to every project.

We manage the process end-to-end: design coordination, permitting, framing, and finishes, so you get a home that's built right the first time. Whether it's a custom home rising from the foundation or a kitchen remodel that changes how a family gathers, our approach doesn't change: plan carefully, build precisely, and keep you informed at every step.

Who's Behind It

Built by brothers.

Prufrock Builders was founded by brothers Florin and Clyde, who grew up around construction and real estate long before it became the family business: it was simply the family. Both studied at Emory University, and that background gave them a rare combination: the on-the-ground instincts of people who've been on job sites their whole lives, and the analytical rigor to run a construction company like it should be run.

What sets them apart isn't just knowledge of the trades: it's how they apply it. Florin and Clyde bring complementary strengths to every project: sharp scheduling that keeps builds on track, and a level of communication that keeps clients informed instead of guessing. It's that combination (technical fluency, disciplined project management, and a genuine commitment to keeping people in the loop) that defines how Prufrock builds.

How We Work

Our process.

  1. Design & Planning

    We start by understanding how you want to live in the space, then coordinate with architects and engineers to turn that into a buildable plan.

  2. Permitting & Budgeting

    We handle the paperwork and set a clear budget up front, so there are no surprises once work begins.

  3. Construction

    From framing to mechanicals to finishes, our crews and trade partners execute the plan with regular progress updates.

  4. Final Walkthrough

    We walk every project with you before calling it done, addressing details until the result matches the plan.
