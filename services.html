What We Do

Services

Whether you're starting from a foundation or reworking every room, Prufrock Builders manages the full scope (design coordination through final finish) for clients across Westchester County, NY and New York City.

01

Ground-Up Construction

Custom homes built from foundation to final finish. We coordinate site work, framing, mechanicals, and finishes into a single, accountable build process.

02

Full-Home Remodeling

Kitchens, bathrooms, and whole-interior renovations. We work within your home's existing structure to deliver spaces that feel new without losing what made the house yours.

03

Additions & Expansions

Need more room? We design and build additions, from a single bump-out to a second story, that tie seamlessly into your existing home.

04

Design-Build Coordination

Architects, engineers, and subcontractors under one roof. We manage the relationships and the handoffs so nothing falls through the cracks.

05

Project Management

Permitting, scheduling, and budget oversight from day one. You get a single point of contact and a clear view of where the project stands at every phase.

06

Roofing

New roof systems on ground-up builds, full tear-offs, and re-roofs on renovations, installed with the same weatherproofing rigor we bring to every exterior detail.

07

Commercial & Office Build-Outs

Full office build-outs and gut renovations in occupied buildings, from demolition through final fit-out, coordinated around building management and tenant schedules.

Ground-Up Construction

Custom homes, built from the foundation up.

When you're starting from bare land or a teardown, every decision compounds: foundation, framing, mechanicals, finishes. We manage the full sequence ourselves, coordinating architects, engineers, and trade partners so the schedule holds and the details don't get lost in translation.

The result is a home built exactly to plan, with nothing improvised along the way.

Remodeling

Full remodels, inside and out.

Whether it's a single room or a whole-house renovation, we handle the interior (kitchens, bathrooms, custom millwork) and the exterior (siding, windows, rooflines) as one coordinated project, not a series of separate jobs.

And when a remodel needs more square footage, we design and build additions that tie in seamlessly, so the new space feels like it was always part of the house.

Roofing

Masterful roofing, on every project.

Roofing is where the details either hold up for decades or fail quietly for years, so we treat it as its own discipline, not an afterthought. Our crews handle asphalt shingle, standing-seam metal, and solar-integrated roof systems, with the flashing, ventilation, and weatherproofing work done right the first time.

Whether it's a new roof framed as part of a ground-up build or a full re-roof on a renovation, it gets the same precision as the rest of the house.

Commercial & Office

Office build-outs, done on a business schedule.

Commercial work runs on a different clock: tenant improvement deadlines, building management coordination, and after-hours access windows all have to be planned around, not worked around. We manage full office build-outs and gut renovations in occupied buildings, from demolition through final fit-out.

Whether it's a full-floor NYC office or a smaller commercial space, the standard doesn't change: clear communication with building management, clean job sites, and a finished space ready for your team to move into.
